Anchored

Two people. One commitment.

Quitting pornography is hard to do alone. Anchored pairs you with one person you trust, so the hard days don’t stay invisible.

For people quitting, and for the partners who show up with them.

Real accountability makes real change more likely. When someone you trust can see your streak, your check-ins, and your SOS alerts, you don’t have to white-knuckle it alone. They don’t see your browsing or your history. Progress is shared, not policed.

How it works

Two seats. One private pair. Honest check-ins without surveillance.

  1. Pick your seat

    Need Anchored for yourself, or joining as someone’s partner? Choose a role, then connect with one other person.

  2. Connect privately

    Pair with a short code. After that, you share a live feed of streaks, check-ins, support, and SOS. Nothing else.

  3. Show up every day

    Track your streak from a quit date. Check in on a simple scale. Press SOS when you need backup. If you slip, you can log it without erasing why you started.

You need Anchored

  • Streak from your quit date

You are someone’s partner

  • See their streak and check-ins in realtime
  • Catch SOS and relapse moments as they happen
  • Log support so they know you showed up
  • Pin a message that stays on their home screen

This is not surveillance

Anchored does not read browsing, watch a device, or pull history. Your partner only sees what you share in the app: streaks, check-ins, and alerts. That way two people can actually show up for each other.
